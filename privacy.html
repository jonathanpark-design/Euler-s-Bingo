Privacy

Prof Says is a free class game. This page explains what it stores. Last updated November 2026.

What we store

When you play, we store an anonymous device ID, the username you choose, and your game activity: votes, words, guesses, taps, chat messages, coins and cosmetics. Game data is visible to other players in the same game.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your real name, school or email unless you choose to sign in with Google. We never save audio.

Microphone

In Pi's Point Rush, the host can turn on phone listening. Listening uses your browser's built-in speech service, which may send audio to your browser vendor to be turned into text. It starts only when you tap the button, and you can stop it any time.

Cookies and ads

The site keeps your sign-in and settings in your browser's local storage. If ads are shown, the ad provider may use cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Services we use

Hosting by GitHub Pages, data storage by Supabase, and domain services by Cloudflare. Each handles data under its own policies.

Removal

Hosts can delete their game, and instructors can ask for a class to be removed. To have your data deleted, contact us at the address below.
